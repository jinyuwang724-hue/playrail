Use case: logo-brand.
Deliver ONE finished 1024x1024 desktop app icon only, no presentation sheet.
Client: Claw, a Windows handheld gaming library and control center. Existing UI: nearly black, elegant cover-art wall with a narrow central opening, restrained panels, soft subtle backgrounds, typography-led and calm. Desired character: simple, restrained, beautiful, distinctive, designed with care.
The previous design (an angular C, a play-triangle cutout, and a yellow fragment) was rejected as unattractive. Start from a fresh construction; do not repeat that mark.
MANDATORY PALETTE: achromatic only: near-black #101114, pure cool white #F3F4F6 and optional neutral/cool gray #BFC3CA. Absolutely no yellow, gold, amber, cream, beige, warm-white, warm metallic tint, or any other chromatic accent.
A strong single graphic silhouette, broad rounded shapes, generous purposeful negative space, optical balance, generous padding. Flat vector-like artwork, front-facing. No letters, wordmark, captions, numbers, watermark, extras, multiple options, device mockups, scenery. No gradients, 3D, bevels, shiny materials, shadows, glow or ornamental details. No gear, wrench, lightning, dragon, animal claws, play triangle, or abstract letter C.
Presentation: one near-black rounded-square app tile about 88 percent of the square canvas, with genuine alpha transparency outside its rounded corners. Mark occupies roughly 53–58 percent of the full canvas. Strong legibility at 32px. All artwork neutral monochrome.
DIRECTION — THE OPEN PAGES:
Design an elegant minimal icon using two softly rounded broad geometric page/card ribbons, slightly splayed like a small collection of game covers opening. A graceful folded-page silhouette with one clean diagonal division, calm upright balance, clean negative-space separation, one pure cool-white front plane and one quiet cool-light-gray back plane. Very little overlap and no detailed folds, so this reads immediately at small sizes. It should evoke curated covers and a beautifully arranged personal library, subtle paper geometry with soft end corners, NOT a literal open-book drawing or several stacked rectangles. Shape-first identity, compact, no text, no play or arrow cutout. The two planes should form a distinctive airy silhouette with one gentle rising diagonal. Warmth must come only from the softened forms, never from color.
